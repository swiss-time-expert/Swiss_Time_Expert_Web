Module 06: Machining Methods, Finishes & High Technology

⚙️ Technologies, Decoration & Silicon

1. The Three Main Stages of the Manufacturing Cycle

Every watch component chronologically follows a rigorous transformation sequence, from raw metal to high horology finishing:

  • 1° Blanking / Roughing (Primary shaping):
    • Rolling: Reducing metal thickness into calibrated strips.
    • Stamping: Plastic deformation under a press.
    • Punching / Cutting: Initial blanking of rough shapes.
  • 2° Dimensional Finishing (Precision machining):
    • Turning & Milling: In conventional methods or on multi-axis CNC machining centers.
    • Electrical Discharge Machining (EDM): Wire EDM for narrow profiles and hardened steels.
  • 3° Aesthetic Finishes & Surface Treatments:
    • Traditional decoration: Côtes de Genève, perlage, hand-bevelling (anglage), mirror polishing.
    • Galvanic treatments: Gilding, rhodium plating, nickel plating for protection and aesthetics.

2. Advanced Technologies & Micro-Manufacturing

  • LIGA / UV-LIGA Technology: Galvanic growth through a photolithographic mold for complex and open-worked nickel-phosphorus parts.
  • Silicon Components: Plasma beam etching (DRIE) for non-magnetic and lubrication-free hairsprings, pallet levers, and escape wheels.
  • Synthetic Rubies and Sapphires: Ultrasonic and diamond machining for gear train bearings and protective crystals.

10. The Balance-Spring: Shaping & Regulation Methods

  • Profiles: Flat Balance-Spring vs Breguet Balance-Spring (overcoil).
  • Geometric comparison of the terminal curve for optimizing the isochronism of oscillations.
  • Regulation Methods: 1° By Regulator, 2° By Variable Inertia (Microstella/Gyromax).

    • Technical Focus: The Swan-Neck Regulator

    • Mechanical Principle: The regulator is held in constant contact against a micrometric screw by a swan-neck shaped elastic blade.
    • Fine Adjustment: By turning the screw, the regulator is moved with extreme gradualness, modifying the active length of the balance spring without risk of slipping.
    • Security: Guarantees superior adjustment precision and firmly holds the regulator against daily shocks.

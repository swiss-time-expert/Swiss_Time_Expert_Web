Constant Force: The Fusée and Chain

This historical mechanism acts as a continuously variable transmission to compensate for the loss of torque from the mainspring as it unwinds.

  • Maximum winding: The spring is strong, the chain pulls on the small diameter of the fusée (short lever arm).
  • Unwinding: The spring weakens, the chain pulls on the large diameter of the fusée (long lever arm), thus perfectly equalizing the force transmitted to the escapement.

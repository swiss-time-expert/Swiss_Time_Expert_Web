2. Motor Organ (Energy): The Barrel, Mainspring & Torque Dynamics

The barrel is the central component for storing potential energy in a mechanical movement. Atelier analysis reveals critical mechanical details regarding mainspring attachment and torque delivery curves.

📝 Atelier Notes: Mainspring Geometry & Torque Curve (Ref. Fig 4.4, p. 46)

1. Mainspring Attachment: The mainspring is held by a bridle (hook) located on the inside wall of the barrel drum. At the center, a hook is positioned on the barrel arbor to secure the inner end of the spring. Furthermore, the inner end of the mainspring features a dedicated fixing hole.

2. Torque Analysis (Arre p. 60, p. 311): During the first turns of winding, the delivered force/torque is remarkably low. Conversely, at full winding (top of the curve), the force is extremely high. This non-linear power curve requires regulation to ensure consistent amplitude across the movement's power reserve.
